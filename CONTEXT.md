# Elsa Control

Elsa Control is the commercial control-plane product in this repository. Its language deliberately distinguishes the product from the separate Elsa Workflows open-source project it integrates with.

## Language

**Elsa Control**:
Commercial deployment-governance and operations software developed and distributed by Valence Works for professional teams using Elsa Workflows.
_Avoid_: Elsa Platform, ElsaPlatform, or other retired product names

**Elsa Workflows**:
The separate, MIT-licensed, vendor-neutral open-source workflow project with which Elsa Control integrates.
_Avoid_: Treating Elsa Workflows as part of Elsa Control or as a Valence Works-owned product

**Valence Works**:
The product and trade identity under which Elsa Control is developed and distributed; this term does not by itself establish the legal licensor.
_Avoid_: Using Valence Works as a legal-entity name without verified legal documentation

**Product-owned identity**:
A name or identifier defined solely by Elsa Control and therefore eligible for the clean product rename.
_Avoid_: Treating Elsa Workflows interoperability names as product-owned

**Elsa Workflows interoperability identity**:
An upstream package, namespace, schema, runtime-family, or integration concept whose Elsa name communicates compatibility with Elsa Workflows.
_Avoid_: Renaming upstream Elsa identities for cosmetic consistency
